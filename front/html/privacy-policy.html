Privacy policy

In compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD), users are informed about how their personal data is processed.

1. Data controller

  • Controller: Roketto development team
  • Email: rokettotaldea@gmail.com

2. Purpose of data processing

The data collected through the platform will be used exclusively for the following purposes:

  1. Volunteer management: registration and sign-up of volunteers to take part in clean-up teams at fixed-location or sports events.
  2. Assignment and monitoring: coordination and tracking of the location/route assigned to volunteers at sports or specific events.
  3. Attendance validation and certification: confirmation of the volunteers' participation and the tasks they carried out.
  4. Contact and portfolio: answering questions received through the corporate website or hiring/employment requests.

3. Legal basis for processing

The legal basis for processing the data is the user's consent, given by ticking the corresponding acceptance boxes in the registration or contact forms.

4. Data retention

The personal data provided will be kept for the academic duration of the DAW 2025-2026 project and until its final assessment, after which it will be permanently deleted or anonymised.

5. Recipients and data transfers

The data will not be transferred to third parties outside the project except where required by law or where expressly needed to organise the event (for example, prior communication with the company or organisation running the sustainable event in order to coordinate the volunteers).

6. Users' rights

Any user may exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection by sending an email to rokettotaldea@gmail.com with a copy of their identity document or equivalent proof of identity.
