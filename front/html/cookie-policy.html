Cookie policy

In compliance with current regulations (LSSI-CE and the guidelines of the Spanish Data Protection Agency, AEPD), users are informed about the use of cookies on this platform.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are downloaded and stored on the user's device when browsing a website, in order to make the platform work or to collect statistical information.

2. Types of cookies used on this website

As this project pursues the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), applying web eco-design criteria and reducing its digital carbon footprint, we keep the use of cookies to a minimum:

  • Technical and strictly necessary cookies: they allow the website to work properly, manage user sessions (volunteers/organisers) and store the authentication status. They do not require prior consent.
  • Preference or personalisation cookies: they store the user's language or interface preferences.
  • Analytics or performance cookies: they are used to measure traffic and use of the platform anonymously.

3. Managing and disabling cookies

Users can configure or disable cookies at any time from their web browser's settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Settings > Privacy.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data.
