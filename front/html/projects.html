These are some of the projects we have developed.

SignaTour

Full Stack application of accessible cultural itineraries for deaf and hard-of-hearing people. It shows routes through museums, streets and monuments with resources such as Spanish Sign Language, subtitles, sign-language guides or hearing loops, and includes login with user and administrator roles.

View project

EcoFestival Bilbao 2024

Development of the official web platform for managing waste and volunteer brigades during the music festival. We coordinated more than 120 volunteers across 4 zones of the venue, collecting over 2 tonnes of plastic for recycling.

View project

Dragones y Mazorcas

Board game search engine and comparison tool that queries the BoardGameGeek API, with more than 100,000 titles. It lets you search, filter by players, age or duration, save favourites, compare them in a table and export them to PDF or CSV, all without reloading the page and with dark mode.

View project

Maratón Verde Donostia

Design and implementation of a section-based tracking system for cleaning up after the race. The application assigned volunteers to specific checkpoints along the 42 km course for the quick removal of cups and packaging from refreshment stations.

View project

Brigadas Azules - Costa Vasca

Coordination platform for groups from companies and schools, focused on collecting microplastics on beaches after water events. It included an interactive dashboard where each brigade can record the kilos of litter removed.

View project

ZeroWaste San Mamés

Pilot project for organising rapid-response volunteering after sporting events. The website distributed cleaning teams across stand sectors at the end of the match, reducing clean-up time by 40%.
